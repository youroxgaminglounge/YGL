YGL CLOUD SYNC AND CUSTOMER BOOKING - SETUP
===========================================
Staff sync lets every staff device that signs in with the same User ID and
password see the same bookings, payments and revenue. Customer booking lets
customers register with their mobile number, book slots on their own phones,
and have those bookings appear in the staff app.
Everything is stored in a Google Sheet that you own.

Easiest on a computer. On a phone, open the Google pages in Chrome and turn on
"Desktop site" from the Chrome menu.

IF YOU ALREADY HAVE THE SERVER (update it)
1. Open your Google Sheet > Extensions > Apps Script.
2. Delete all the code, paste the full contents of the new Code.gs, press Save.
3. Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil icon > Version: New version > Deploy.
   The web app address stays the same. Do not create a new deployment.
4. Do the TIME ZONE step below.
Then go to PART 3 (customer booking).

PART 1 - CREATE THE SERVER (first time only)
1. Go to sheets.google.com and create a new blank spreadsheet. Name it "YGL Data".
2. Menu: Extensions > Apps Script. A code editor opens.
3. Select everything in the editor, delete it, then paste the full contents of
   Code.gs from this folder. Press Save.
4. Press Deploy > New deployment. Click the gear icon and choose "Web app".
     Execute as: Me
     Who has access: Anyone
   Press Deploy.
5. Press "Authorize access", choose your Google account. If Google says the app
   is not verified, choose Advanced > "Go to project (unsafe)" and Allow.
   (It is your own script, so this is expected.)
6. Copy the "Web app URL" (it ends with /exec). Keep it private.

TIME ZONE (important for customer booking)
In Apps Script click the gear icon (Project settings) and set the Time zone to
your lounge's time zone, for example (GMT+05:30) India Standard Time. The server
uses it to check opening hours.

PART 2 - STAFF SIGN-IN (every staff device)
1. Open the YGL app. Tap "Sign in to sync" at the top.
2. First device: tap "Create account", paste the Web app URL, choose a User ID and
   password. Other devices: "Log in" with the same details.
The FIRST account created is treated as the lounge owner. Customer bookings go into
that account's data. To use a different staff account, set LOUNGE_USER at the top
of Code.gs to its User ID.

PART 3 - CUSTOMER BOOKING
1. In the staff app open Setup > Online booking. Choose how many days ahead
   customers can book, the minimum notice and the longest booking. Press Save setup.
2. Tap "Copy link". This is your customer booking link. Share it on WhatsApp or
   Instagram, or paste it into any free QR code maker and print the QR at the counter.
3. Customers open the link, tap Register, enter their name, mobile number and a
   4 to 6 digit PIN, choose a day, a station and a start time, and tap Book.
   They can cancel their own upcoming bookings from the same page.
4. Staff see the bookings automatically (after the app syncs, usually within seconds):
   the Slots grid shows them as "Online", and the Floor shows them with the customer's
   number when it is time to check in. The "Guests" tab lists every registered
   customer with their number and number of bookings.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Two customers can never take the same slot: the server checks every booking.
- A customer can hold up to 3 upcoming bookings (change CUSTOMER_MAX_UPCOMING in Code.gs).
- Bookings are not paid online. Customers pay at the counter.
- Mobile numbers are not verified by SMS. Check the number at the counter, and use the
  Block button in the Guests tab for anyone who misuses online booking.
- Switch online booking off any time: Setup > Online booking > "No, switched off".
- Forgot-PIN for a customer: open the Customers tab of the Google Sheet and delete
  that customer's row. They can register again with the same number.
- Forgot-password for staff: delete that user's row in the Users tab and create the
  account again with the same User ID. The data is kept in the d_<userid> tab.
- If a customer's phone is on a different time zone from the lounge, slot times
  they see may be off. Customers should be in the lounge's time zone.
- Do not edit the d_ tabs by hand. You can read them and back them up.
- The Google Play version of the app stores data only on the phone and has no sync
  or customer booking.
