Privacy Policy

YGL Book · Yourox Gaming Lounge · Effective 25 October 2026

What this app does

YGL Book lets you sign up, choose a lounge branch, check which gaming slots are free, and book and pay for a slot online.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and mobile number, and a PIN you choose. The PIN is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so we cannot read it.
  • Booking details: the branch, station, date, time, number of players and the amount for each booking.
  • Payment status: whether a payment succeeded, its payment reference number and the amount.

We do not see or store your card number, UPI PIN, bank details or CVV. Payments are handled by our payment provider, Razorpay, on its own secure payment window.

How we use it

We use your details to manage your account, reserve your slot, confirm your payment, let the branch staff identify you at the counter, and handle refunds or problems with a booking. We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

  • Other customers never see your details. They only see that a slot is taken.
  • The branch you book at (its staff and owner) can see your name, mobile number and booking details.
  • Razorpay processes your payment and receives the details needed for that. Its own privacy policy applies to the payment.
  • Google provides the Google Sheets and Apps Script service where the lounge keeps its booking records.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

Your information is stored in the lounge’s Google Sheet. It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is limited to the lounge owner and branch staff.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: log in, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account, then enter your PIN. This removes your name and mobile number from your account and from the branch’s customer list. We keep booking and payment records (without your name on the customer list) for as long as needed for accounting, refunds and legal reasons. You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing us at the address below.

Children

This app is meant for adults. Children should book with a parent or guardian.
