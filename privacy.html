Privacy Policy

YGL Gaming Lounge Manager · Yourox Gaming Lounge · Effective 23 October 2026

What this app does

YGL Gaming Lounge Manager helps a gaming lounge manage station slots, guest check-in and check-out, billing and daily revenue.

Information the app stores

When you use the app you may type in guest names, phone numbers, number of players, booking times, entry and exit times, amounts charged, amounts received, payment method (cash, UPI or card), station names and rates, and revenue totals for previous days.

Where it is stored

All of this information is stored only on your own device, inside the app. We do not run a server for this app, we do not receive your data, and we cannot see it. The app has no user accounts.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your data.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app does not ask for permissions such as location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

Optional cloud sync (web version only)

The Android app from Google Play stores everything only on your device and has no sync feature. The separate web version of the app offers an optional sign-in that copies your records to a Google Sheet that the lounge owner controls, so several devices can show the same data. If you use that option, your records are stored in that owner’s Google account, not with us.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your records stay on the device until you delete them. You can erase all of them by clearing the app’s storage in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app. Because the data is only on your device, we cannot recover it for you if it is deleted or the device is lost, so please keep your own backup of important totals.

Guests’ information

If you enter your customers’ names or phone numbers, you are responsible for handling that information properly and in line with the laws that apply to your business.

Children

The app is a business tool for lounge staff and is not directed at children.
